# Verification pass, 2026-10-02

Every row below was read from the provider's own page on 2026-10-02 and
compared with the value the catalogue uses. `sources/2026-10-02/MANIFEST.tsv`
pins the URL, the HTTP status, the byte count and the SHA-256 of the copy read.

## scaleway-stardust — NEW, verified

- Source: `https://www.scaleway.com/en/pricing/virtual-instances/` (200, 6,782,563 bytes).
- Quote (rendered table): `STARDUST1-S | 1 vCPU | 1 GB | 100 Mbps | €0.0006 / HOUR`.
- Quote (embedded catalogue JSON): `"apiId":"STARDUST1-S","catalogId":"p_instance_stardust-s","hardware":{...,"cpu":{"arch":"x64","sharedCpu":true,"type":"AMD EPYC™ 7281 (2.1 GHz) or equivalent","virtual":{"count":1}}...,"ram":{"...","size":1073741824...},"storages":[{"count":1,"dynamicLocal":{"maxSize":10000000000,"type":"SSD"}}...]}` and
  `"price":{"hourly":{"sku":"/instance/server/stardust1_s/fr-par-1","value":{"currencyCode":"EUR","nanos":600000,"units":0}},"monthly":{"...nanos":438000000...}}`.
- Arithmetic: €0.0006/h × 1.1378 = $0.00068268/h; × 730 h = **$0.498/mo**.
- Verdict: **confirms the addition**. The battleships `scaleway.json` card
  omits this tier entirely (its cheapest is DEV1-S at ~€0.0102/h, ~17× the
  Stardust rate). Scaleway's true entry price was missing from the corpus.
- Caveat that keeps it honest: 1 shared vCPU / 1 GiB / 10 GB local SSD, EU only,
  hourly billing with a one-hour minimum, IPv4 is a separate charge
  (card `ipv4_month` $4.15), no monthly cap.

- Quote (vps-lite): `VPS Lite 1 G12.5s 2 vCore (x86) 4 GB RAM 80 GB SSD ... 6 months contract period, from monthly €5.86 (incl. 19% VAT)`.
- Quote (vps): `VPS 500 G12.5 2 vCore (x86) 4 GB RAM ... 12 months contract period, from monthly €8.26 €9.50 (incl. 19% VAT)`.
- Arithmetic: €5.86 incl. 19% VAT = €4.92 net → $5.60; the card encards

  says "No preference Europe". Either figure keeps netcup at rank 1 for
  `agent-box`, so the headline does not move, but the number is a **lower
  bound**, not a confirmed price.

## Search note (negative result)

this sandbox**: `lite.duckduckgo.com`, `html.duckduckgo.com` and `bing.com`
all returned JavaScript/consent shells with no machine-readable result list

index (366 providers) plus direct first-party pages. This is a limit of the
route, not a claim that the list is complete.
